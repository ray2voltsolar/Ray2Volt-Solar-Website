Net metering One meter that counts in both directions

A solar plant supplies its own building first. Power that is not needed at that moment flows out to the grid, and power that is still needed flows in. One two-way meter records both, and the bill is raised only on the difference.

Units billed for the month

At an illustrative ₹7 per unit, the bill falls from ₹3,500 to ₹980. Each exported unit cancels an imported unit at the full retail rate. Real tariffs have slabs and fixed charges.

Net metering lets the midday surplus offset the morning and evening shortfall.

If export exceeds import

Depending on the state, the surplus is carried forward as a credit or paid out at a rate fixed by the regulator, usually below the retail tariff.

Limits to know

Size limits, settlement periods and surplus rates are set by each State Electricity Regulatory Commission. In Andhra Pradesh: up to 500 kW for new systems, settled monthly.

Where it is typically used

Wherever much of the solar power is used on site: homes, shops, offices, schools, hospitals and small industry.
