Sharing solar: group and virtual net metering

Sometimes the best roof is not above the building that uses the power, or one roof serves many households. Two arrangements let a single plant's units be credited on other electricity bills, in shares agreed in advance.

Group net metering

One owner, several connections

The plant's surplus is adjusted against the same owner's other connections in the same DISCOM area, for example a trust with a school, a hostel and an office.

Virtual net metering

One plant, several consumers

One shared plant's output is credited to different consumers in agreed shares, for example flats in a block whose only roof is the common terrace.

Only where your state allows it

Group and virtual net metering exist only where the state regulation provides for them. Your State Electricity Regulatory Commission sets the terms, and your DISCOM decides how they are applied. Confirm current rules with your DISCOM before you decide.

Subsidy

Group and virtual net-metered rooftop systems can qualify for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy where the DISCOM permits them. The subsidy is for homes only, not for commercial or industrial connections.
