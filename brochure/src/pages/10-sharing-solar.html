Sharing solar Group and virtual net metering

Sometimes the best roof is not above the building that uses the power, or one roof serves many households. Two arrangements let one plant's units be credited on other electricity bills, in shares set in advance.

Exhibit 1  ·  Group net metering Illustrative

One owner, several connections. A trust's plant on its school roof serves the school first; the surplus is adjusted against the same trust's hostel and office connections in the same DISCOM area.

Exhibit 2  ·  Virtual net metering Illustrative

One plant, several consumers. A shared plant on an apartment block's common terrace; each flat, a separate consumer with no roof of its own, receives an agreed share on its own bill.

Only where the state allows it

Group and virtual net metering exist only where the state regulation provides for them, on terms set by the State Electricity Regulatory Commission. Confirm current rules with your DISCOM before you decide.

Subsidy

Group and virtual net-metered rooftop systems can qualify for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy where the DISCOM permits them. The subsidy is for homes only.

Figures are illustrative. How surplus is calculated and how shares are set and changed follow each state's regulation.
