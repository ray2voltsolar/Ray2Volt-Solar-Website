Paying for it Subsidy, loans and tax

Homes can claim a central subsidy and borrow on concessional terms. Businesses cannot claim the home subsidy, but can borrow and may benefit from tax depreciation.

As of November 2026. Schemes and rates change; confirm with your bank and the official portal, pmsuryaghar.gov.in.

Exhibit 1  ·  Home subsidy by plant size ₹ per home
₹30,000 a kW, first 2 kW ₹18,000, third kW No more above 3 kW

Housing societies and resident welfare associations: ₹18,000 per kW for common facilities, including EV charging, up to 500 kW (at 3 kW per house). The scheme is currently sanctioned until March 2027.

Exhibit 2  ·  How it is paid Process
  1. Apply online, only on pmsuryaghar.gov.in, for a home with a residential electricity connection.
  2. A registered vendor installs a grid-connected rooftop system with made-in-India panels from the government’s approved list.
  3. Inspection and net meter; the plant is commissioned.
  4. Subsidy paid into the applicant’s bank account.

Homes only. The subsidy is not available to commercial or industrial connections.
