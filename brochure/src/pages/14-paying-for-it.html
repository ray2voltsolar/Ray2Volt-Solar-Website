Paying for it Subsidy, loans and tax

Homes can claim a central government subsidy and borrow on concessional terms. Businesses cannot claim the home subsidy, but can borrow and may benefit from tax rules on depreciation.

As of November 2026. Schemes and rates change. Confirm with your bank and the official portal, pmsuryaghar.gov.in, before you decide.

Exhibit 1  ·  PM Surya Ghar subsidy by plant size ₹ per home
₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW ₹18,000 for the third kW No further subsidy

Central scheme, currently sanctioned until March 2027. Target: 1 crore households; over 65 lakh homes covered by November 2026.

Conditions

  • A residential electricity connection
  • A grid-connected rooftop system
  • Made-in-India panels from the government’s approved list
  • Installation by a registered vendor

Housing societies

Housing societies and resident welfare associations: ₹18,000 per kW for common facilities, including EV charging, up to 500 kW (at 3 kW per house).

Homes only. The subsidy is not available to commercial or industrial connections.
