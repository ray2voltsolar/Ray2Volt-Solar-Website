Paying for it: subsidy, loans and tax

Homes can get a central government subsidy and a bank loan on easy terms. Businesses cannot get the home subsidy, but can borrow and may save on tax. Here is what is available today.

As of November 2026Schemes and rates change. Confirm with your bank and the official portal, pmsuryaghar.gov.in, before you decide.

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana Central subsidy for home rooftop solar

Conditions

  • A residential electricity connection
  • A grid-connected rooftop system
  • Made-in-India panels from the government’s approved list
  • Installed by a registered vendor

Housing societies and resident welfare associations: ₹18,000 per kW for common facilities, including EV charging, up to 500 kW (at 3 kW per house).

Homes only. The subsidy is not for commercial or industrial connections such as shops, offices and factories.

A central government scheme, currently sanctioned until March 2027. Target: 1 crore households; over 65 lakh homes covered by November 2026.

Loans, by type of borrower

Lenders typically fund about 70–80% of a business project’s cost

Homes

  • Public-sector banks: collateral-free loans for systems up to 3 kW (up to about ₹2 lakh); larger loans up to 10 kW
  • Tenure up to 10 years, at a concessional interest rate linked to the RBI repo rate; ask your bank for the current rate
  • Apply through JanSamarth (jansamarth.in), linked from the national portal

Micro, small and medium enterprises

  • Term loans from banks and NBFCs (non-bank finance companies)
  • SIDBI rooftop-solar loans for MSMEs
  • CGTMSE credit-guarantee cover for micro and small enterprises, up to ₹10 crore from April 2025

Larger businesses

  • Term loans from banks and NBFCs
  • IREDA, a government renewable-energy lender
  • Or no loan at all, through the OPEX / RESCO route on page 13

For lenders · priority sector. Under RBI’s priority sector lending directions (2025), renewable-energy loans to individual households count up to ₹10 lakh per borrower, and loans to renewable power generators up to ₹35 crore.
