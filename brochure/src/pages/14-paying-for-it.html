Paying for it Subsidy, loans and tax

Homes can claim a central subsidy and borrow on concessional terms. Businesses cannot claim the home subsidy, but can borrow and may benefit from tax depreciation.

As of November 2026. Schemes and rates change; confirm with your bank and the official portal, pmsuryaghar.gov.in.

Exhibit 1  ·  Home subsidy by plant size ₹ per home
₹30,000 per kW, first 2 kW ₹18,000, third kW No further amount

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana. Sanctioned until March 2027; target 1 crore homes, over 65 lakh covered.

Conditions

  • A residential electricity connection
  • A grid-connected rooftop system
  • Made-in-India panels from the government’s approved list
  • Installation by a registered vendor

Housing societies

Housing societies and resident welfare associations get ₹18,000 per kW for common facilities, including EV charging, up to 500 kW (at 3 kW per house).

Homes only. The subsidy is not available to commercial or industrial connections.
