Buying green power at scale

Open access: buying power from someone other than your local supplier

Normally all your electricity comes from the DISCOM, your local distribution company. Open access lets a large consumer buy power from another seller, or from its own plant elsewhere, and use the existing grid to deliver it. Think of a courier: you choose who you buy from, the network already in place carries it, and you pay for the delivery.

Who can use it

From 100 kW, down from 1 MW

National rules and state regulations allow consumers with 100 kW or more of sanctioned load (the maximum load a connection is approved for) to buy green power through open access, on terms set by each state. That can be one connection, or several in the same DISCOM electricity division adding up to 100 kW. Captive users, who own their plant, have no minimum.

The alternative: a green tariff

A consumer who cannot or does not want to use open access can ask the DISCOM to supply green power at a “green tariff” set by the State Electricity Regulatory Commission.

Confirm current rules with your DISCOM before you decide.
