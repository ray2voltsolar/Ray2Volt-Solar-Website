Exhibit 4  ·  Monthly settlement Schematic

Homes up to 10 kW

Under PM Surya Ghar, domestic consumers pay no application fee or net-meter cost; the feasibility study is waived.

Rooftop regulation

APERC Regulation 4 of 2023, amended by Regulation 10 of 2025. Zero-export behind-the-meter systems follow a simplified intimation process.

Green open access

APERC Regulation 3 of 2024 (amended 2025). Under the AP Integrated Clean Energy Policy 2024, cross-subsidy surcharge and additional surcharge do not apply to captive consumption.
