The rules behind the numbers

  • Rooftop solar: APERC Regulation 4 of 2023, amended by Regulation 10 of 2025. Zero-export behind-the-meter systems follow a simplified intimation process.
  • Green open access: APERC Regulation 3 of 2024 (amended 2025). Under the AP Integrated Clean Energy Policy 2024, cross-subsidy surcharge and additional surcharge do not apply to captive consumption.

Rules change often. These are the Andhra Pradesh rules as of November 2026. Confirm current rules with your DISCOM before you decide.
