Exhibit 1  ·  Two words you need: kW and kWh Power × time = energy

kW · a rate

A kilowatt is how fast power flows at a moment, like a car’s speed. A plant’s size is quoted in kW (often written kWp).

kWh · an amount

A kilowatt-hour is energy made or used over time, like distance driven. 1 kWh is the “unit” on a bill.
