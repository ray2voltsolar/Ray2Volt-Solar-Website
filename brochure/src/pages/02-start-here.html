Two words you need

kW is how fast power flows. A unit (kWh) is how much you use.

kW

Kilowatt: a rate. How fast power flows, like a car’s speed. A plant’s size is quoted in kW (often kWp).

kWh

Kilowatt-hour: an amount. Energy made or used, like distance driven. 1 kWh is the “unit” on your bill.
