How solar makes electricity: from sunlight to socket

Solar panels turn daylight into electricity with no moving parts and no fuel. An inverter converts that power into the kind your appliances use, and your building uses it first. The grid stays connected alongside, taking any surplus and supplying whatever solar cannot.

The journey · five stages

A plain grid-connected system switches itself off in a power cut, so no power is sent into lines that linemen may be repairing. Systems with batteries are explained on page 05.
