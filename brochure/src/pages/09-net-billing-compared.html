Net billing The same wiring as net metering, settled in rupees

Net billing, also called net feed-in, is wired like net metering: solar serves the building first and the surplus goes out. But imports are priced at the retail tariff, exports at the lower feed-in tariff, and one amount is subtracted from the other.

Under net metering an exported unit cancels a unit worth ₹7. Under net billing it earns the feed-in rate, ₹3 here, so the same plant saves less, and every unit used directly is worth more than one exported.
