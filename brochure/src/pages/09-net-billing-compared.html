Net billing: the same wiring, settled in rupees

Net billing, also called net feed-in, is wired like net metering: solar serves the building first and the surplus goes out. The difference is the settlement. Imports are priced at the retail tariff, exports at the lower feed-in tariff, and one is subtracted from the other.

Read the comparison with care

  • Rates are illustrative: ₹7 a unit from the grid, ₹3 a unit for exports.
  • Real tariffs have slabs and fixed charges, so real bills differ.
  • The result depends on how much solar the building uses directly.
  • Which methods are on offer, and at what size, is set by your State Electricity Regulatory Commission. Confirm current rules with your DISCOM before you decide.
