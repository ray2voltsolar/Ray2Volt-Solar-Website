A consumer can own the plant, share one with others, or simply buy its power. The route decides which charges are added on the way to the meter.

Banking terms

  • Drawn back within the same billing cycle; national rules allow at least monthly banking.
  • National rules allow banking of at least 30%; Andhra Pradesh allows up to 30%.
  • Charge taken in kind: 8% in Andhra Pradesh.
  • Unused units are generally not carried forward.

Who it suits

Typically viable for consumers with large, steady daytime demand, on long-term agreements, often 10 years or more.

What it saves

Savings depend on state charges, voltage level and consumption pattern; ask for a site-specific estimate.

Before you decide

Terms vary by state and change often. Confirm current rules with your DISCOM before you decide.
