About the information in this guide

This guide is for general education. It describes rules, schemes and typical practice in India as understood in November 2026. Electricity regulations differ from state to state and are revised often; subsidy schemes, loan terms and tax rules also change. Worked examples use illustrative rates and are not estimates for any particular consumer. Nothing here is financial, legal or tax advice, or an offer of any investment or loan. Before deciding, confirm current rules with your electricity distribution company, your bank and your chartered accountant, and ask for a written proposal specific to your site.

Based on published material from the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy, the Ministry of Power, the Central Electricity Authority, the Andhra Pradesh Electricity Regulatory Commission, the Reserve Bank of India and the PM Surya Ghar national portal. © 2026 Ray2Volt Solar Private Limited.
