Green energy What it is, and why it matters

Green energy is electricity from sources that nature replenishes and that emit little or no carbon dioxide (CO₂) while running: solar, wind, hydro and biomass, and storage or green hydrogen charged from them. Fossil power burns coal, gas or oil, which are used up.

Why it matters · 01

Steadier costs

Sunlight and wind come with no fuel bill. Once a plant is built, the cost of each unit it makes does not rise and fall with coal or gas prices.

Why it matters · 02

Cleaner air, lower emissions

India’s grid emits about 0.68 kg of CO₂ per unit on average (Central Electricity Authority, FY 2025-26). Each kW of solar avoids about 1 tonne a year.

Why it matters · 03

Energy security

Sunshine and wind are home-grown. Power made on a roof is used where it is made, with no fuel to mine or transport and less lost along the wires.
